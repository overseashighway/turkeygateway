QUICK ANSWERS

Frequently asked questions.

Useful information before you begin.

Can international students study in English in Türkiye?

Some universities offer English-taught programs. The language of instruction, proof-of-language requirements and program availability must be checked for each university and intake.

Are scholarships available for international students?

Scholarships may be offered by government programs or individual universities, subject to competitive selection and current eligibility criteria. Refer to official sources before applying.

Can Turkey Gateway guarantee a visa?

No. Visa decisions are made by the relevant Turkish authorities. We can help explain steps and prepare an application checklist based on published rules.

How do I ask for a personalised consultation?

Use the enquiry form below to open WhatsApp with a prefilled message that includes your interest and contact details. Please avoid sharing passport numbers or sensitive documents in the initial message.
